Explore from realm to room.

Scroll to move smoothly between world, continent, province, settlement, and house detail. The same detail level opens across the whole atlas, so drag sideways to explore neighboring places. Scroll out to reverse the journey. Double-click a place or use the atlas list to fly there.

Use Connect places, then choose two places on the same map to draw a road or other route. Click a route to edit its details.
